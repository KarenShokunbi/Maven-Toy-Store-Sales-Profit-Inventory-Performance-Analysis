# Maven-Toy-Store-Sales-Profit-Inventory-Performance-Analysis
This project analyses transactional sales data from a toy company (2022–2023) to uncover revenue trends, customer purchasing behaviour and track inventory. The dashboard uses Power BI’s data modelling capabilities to deliver actionable insights that support strategic decision-making

## Objectives
Analyse overall sales and profitability performance.
Identify revenue and sales trends across 2022–2023.
Identify best-selling products and product categories.
Monitor inventory levels against customer demand.
Compare performance across stores and store locations.
Provide actionable insights to support business decision-making.

## Dataset Information
Source: Maven Analytics Toy store company dataset
Format: CSV
Time Period: 2022 – 2023
Geographical Scope: Mexico

## Tools and Technologies
Power BI – Data modelling, dashboard development, and DAX
Power Query – Data cleaning and transformation
DAX – Measures, calculated columns.
Visuals – Power BI visuals and Kelly kolosko sparklines
CSV – Raw data source

## Key Metrics
Revenue: Total sales generated.
Profit: Total profit generated from sales.
Quantity Sold: Total units sold.
Total Orders: Number of orders/transactions.
Available Stock: Inventory currently available.
Stock at Hand: Total inventory held.
Store Count: Number of stores analysed.
Total Products: Number of products analysed.

## Key Features
KPI Cards – Provides a quick overview of business performance.
Revenue Trend – Tracks monthly revenue performance across 2022–2023.
Best-Selling Products – Ranks products based on quantity sold.
Orders vs Available Stock – Compares demand with inventory availability.
Revenue vs Profit – Compares financial performance across product categories.
Store Performance Table – Provides store-level sales, stock, revenue and profit analysis.
Sparklines – Shows revenue and profit trends for individual stores.
Location Segmentation – Allows analysis across Airport, Commercial, Downtown and Residential stores.

## Key Insights
The business generated $14.44M in revenue and $4.01M in profit during the analysis period.
Over 1.09M units were sold across 35 products and 50 stores.
Toys generated the highest revenue among the product categories.
Deck of Cards was the highest-selling product by quantity.
Revenue showed noticeable fluctuations across the months, with strong performance toward the end of 2022.
Inventory levels can be compared against order volumes to identify potential stock replenishment needs.
Store and location-level analysis reveals differences in sales and profitability across the business.

## Interactivity
The dashboard allows users to:
Filter performance by store location type.
Explore revenue trends across different months and years.
Interact with product and category visuals.
Compare individual store performance.
Cross-filter visuals to investigate specific segments.
Dynamically update KPIs based on selected filters.

## How to use
Download the .pbix file from this repository
Open using Power BI Desktop
Start with the KPI cards to understand overall business performance.
Use the location filters to analyse specific store types.
Review the revenue trend to identify changes in monthly performance.
Check best-selling products to understand product demand.
Compare orders with available stock to monitor inventory pressure.
Review the store table to identify high- and low-performing stores.
Use the category analysis to compare revenue and profitability.
